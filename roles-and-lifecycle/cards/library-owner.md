# Library owner

Whoever owns the Laya-ADLC library as a whole. One named person, with a deputy.

## What you own

- Keeping the library correct, current and in one place.
- Reviewing changes, and the release of each version.
- Re-checking sources each quarter, because Microsoft and GitHub change often.
- Running the [rollout](../../ROLLOUT.md), and the network of AI champions.

## What AI can do for you

- Draft updates, check links and spot stale facts.
- Summarise feedback from champions.

## What you must not hand over

- Approving new third-party skills: a second person always reviews them.
- Deciding what's allowed: that's for governance.

## Your tools

- The `skill-intake` skill, for anything from outside Laya.
- `tools/check_library.py`, `validate_skills.py` and the tests.

## The evidence you leave

- The changelog, and a list of what's open.
- Sources with their check dates.
