# Team lead or manager

Anyone with people or a team's work to answer for.

## What you own

- Your team's output, including work AI helped with.
- Making clear what your team uses AI for, and what it doesn't.
- Making it safe to report mistakes quickly.
- Making sure everyone has done the AI basics, and has time to learn.

## What AI can do for you

- Help you plan, write and catch up.
- Draft your team's note on how you use AI.
- Prepare a team session.

## What you must not hand over

- Every decision about a person: performance, pay, prospects, discipline.
- Monitoring staff, or analysing their messages or tone.
- Your accountability for the team's work.

## Your tools

- The [team leads playbook](../../non-technical/by-role/team-leads-and-managers.md).
- The [30-minute team session](../../non-technical/training/run-a-team-session.md).
- The Laya Privacy & AI Check, for new uses.

## The evidence you leave

- A short team note on what you use AI for.
- AI uses beyond personal drafting, listed on the register.

## Watch out for

- A process only licensed people can follow. Most of your team may be on the free version.
- Pressure to go faster, which quietly removes the checking.
