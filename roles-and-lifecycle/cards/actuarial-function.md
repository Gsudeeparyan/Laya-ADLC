# Actuarial function

EIOPA says the actuarial function is responsible for controls on AI that falls within its responsibilities, such as technical provisions and the opinion on underwriting policy (paragraph 3.30).

## What you own

- Controls on AI touching reserving, underwriting or pricing.
- Validating any such model independently.

## What AI can do for you

- Document methods, and explain workbooks.
- Prepare data, and do first-pass checks.

## What you must not hand over

- Actuarial judgement and sign-off.
- Using AI for pricing or risk assessment of individuals, without going through the T3 route.

## Your tools

- The [pricing and underwriting playbook](../../non-technical/by-role/pricing-and-underwriting.md).

## The evidence you leave

- Validation reports, and reproducible records of data and method (paragraph 3.23).
