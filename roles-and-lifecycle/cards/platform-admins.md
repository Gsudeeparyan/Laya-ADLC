# Platform admins

Whoever administers Microsoft 365 and GitHub. You turn decisions into settings that hold no matter what anyone types.

## What you own

- Microsoft 365: agent access and sharing, Purview, audit, retention, licences.
- GitHub: Copilot policies, MCP allowlist, cloud agent settings, rulesets, audit logs.
- Keeping settings in line with what governance decided.

## What AI can do for you

- Explain a setting, and draft change notes.

## What you must not hand over

- Deciding what's allowed: governance decides, and you implement.
- Changing a setting without a record.

## Your tools

- [Microsoft 365 settings](../../non-technical/admin/tenant-settings.md).
- [GitHub settings](../../technical/harness/org-admin-checklist.md).

- Change records and audit logs.
