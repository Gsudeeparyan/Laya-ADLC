# Business owner

Whoever owns a process, product or the outcome of a change.

## What you own

- Whether a change is worth making, and what "good" means.
- Requirements, and accepting the result.
- The risk tier, and getting the right sign-offs.
- How a change affects members and staff.

## What AI can do for you

- Shape the idea, and challenge it.
- Draft requirements for IT or a supplier.
- Map the current process and find the slack.

## What you must not hand over

- The decision to go ahead, and to accept the result.
- Commitments to members, staff or suppliers.
- Risk acceptance on Laya's behalf.

## Your tools

- Laya Idea Refiner, Laya Requirements Writer, Laya Process & SOP Builder, Laya Project Planner.
- The [lifecycle](../lifecycle.md) and [risk tiers](../risk-tiers.md).

## The evidence you leave

- A one-page brief, agreed requirements and your acceptance.
- An entry on the AI use register, where one is needed.
