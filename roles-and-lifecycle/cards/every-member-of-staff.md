# Every member of staff

Anyone at Laya who uses Copilot, on the free version or the paid licence.

## What you own

- Everything you send, save or rely on, whoever or whatever drafted it.
- Checking AI's work, in proportion to how much it matters.
- Keeping personal data out of chats unless the job needs it.
- Reporting mistakes the same day.

## What AI can do for you

- Draft, rewrite, summarise and explain.
- Build and change spreadsheets through the Laya agents.
- Find what a document says, when you give it the document.
- Check a draft against its source.

## What you must not hand over

- Decisions about people: claims, cover, prices, complaints, jobs and performance.
- Your judgement on whether something is true, fair and right to send.
- Reading the result before you use it.

## Your tools

- Start with the [Laya AI Helper](../../non-technical/README.md).
- Your department's [playbook](../../non-technical/by-role/README.md).
- Laya Prompt Coach, Laya Second Check, Laya PII Guard, Laya Privacy & AI Check.
- The [AI basics](../../non-technical/training/ai-basics.md) and [one-page rules](../../non-technical/training/one-page-rules.md).

## The evidence you leave

- For normal work: nothing beyond the work itself. Chats are logged automatically.
- For a T2 use: who checked it. For a new use of personal data: the Laya Privacy & AI Check summary.

## Watch out for

- Getting used to trusting it. The more often it's right, the less carefully we check.
- Using a personal account or another AI website for work.
