# Security engineer

Whoever reviews for security, including the risks AI brings.

## What you own

- Threat models and security reviews.
- Rules for what agents may reach: tools, tokens, networks and MCP servers.
- Responding when an agent is involved in an incident.

## What AI can do for you

- A first review pass, finding common failures.
- Explain code quickly, and search for risky patterns.

## What you must not hand over

- The verdict: AI reports, and you decide.
- Accepting risk, which goes to the named owner.

## Your tools

- `security-review`, `skill-intake`, `incident-response`.
- Laya Security Reviewer.
- [Security for AI](../../best-practices/05-security-for-ai.md).

## The evidence you leave

- Review findings with `file:line`, and what wasn't reviewed.

## Watch out for

- Prompt injection through issues, comments and documents.
- Agents holding more access than the task needs.
