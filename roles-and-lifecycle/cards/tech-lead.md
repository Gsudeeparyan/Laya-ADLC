# Tech lead

Whoever sets standards and owns a system's design.

## What you own

- Design decisions, and recording those that are hard to undo.
- `CODING_STANDARDS.md`, and what each review looks for.
- Keeping the codebase easy for agents and people to work in.
- Reviewing the riskiest changes.

## What AI can do for you

- Explore design options, and challenge your own.
- Find where the code could be made simpler.
- Do a first review pass.

## What you must not hand over

- Choosing the design.
- Final review of T2 and T3 changes.
- Standards: AI can suggest, but you decide.

## Your tools

- `codebase-design`, `bounded-contexts`, `improve-codebase-architecture`, `/code-review`, `/retro`.
- Laya Reviewer.

## The evidence you leave

- Decision records (ADRs) and code reviews.

## Watch out for

- More code than anyone can review. Review capacity is the real limit now, so keep batches small.
