# Service owner

Whoever answers for a service in production.

## What you own

- Approving releases.
- Monitoring and on-call.
- Running incidents, and the regulatory clocks that go with them.

## What AI can do for you

- Gather evidence for a release.
- Build a timeline during an incident, and draft updates.
- Suggest causes for you to test.

## What you must not hand over

- Approving the release.
- Decisions during an incident.
- Deciding on notifications: those go with the DPO and Compliance.

## Your tools

- `release-change`, `incident-response`, `production-ready`.
- Laya Release Manager, Laya Incident Responder.

## The evidence you leave

- Change records tied to an exact commit.
- Incident logs and blameless reviews.
