# Agent owner

Whoever owns one Laya agent or skill. Usually someone from the team that relies on it most.

## What you own

- That the agent does its job well and safely.
- Testing it before each change goes live.
- Acting on problems people report.
- Deciding when it should be retired.

## What AI can do for you

- Draft changes to the skill.
- Role-play conversations against it, to find where it goes wrong.

## What you must not hand over

- Approving your own change: someone else reviews it.
- Testing in real Copilot before people rely on it.

## Your tools

- [Writing skills](../../best-practices/04-writing-skills.md).
- The `adlc-agent-author` skill in this library.
- The [smoke test](../../non-technical/admin/smoke-test.md).

## The evidence you leave

- Version number and change history, in the library.
- Smoke test results.
