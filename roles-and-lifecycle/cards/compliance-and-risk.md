# Compliance and risk

EIOPA says the compliance and audit functions verify that AI use complies with applicable laws and regulations (paragraph 3.30).

## What you own

- Interpreting regulation for Laya, and maintaining [regulation](../../best-practices/07-regulation.md).
- Fitting AI into the risk framework, not running it on the side.
- Reviewing AI that reaches members.
- Mapping which duties sit with Laya and which with AXA.

## What AI can do for you

- Summarise regulations you give it, and compare them with a policy.
- Keep registers.

## What you must not hand over

- Every interpretation. AI invents citations that look real.
- Anything sent to a regulator, unchecked.

## Your tools

- The [Compliance playbook](../../non-technical/by-role/compliance-risk-and-legal.md).
- Laya Document Summariser, Laya Privacy & AI Check.

## The evidence you leave

- Your review of this library.
- Reviews of AI uses, and how issues were closed.
