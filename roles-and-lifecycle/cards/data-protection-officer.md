# Data Protection Officer

EIOPA says the DPO verifies that personal data processed by AI systems complies with data protection law (paragraph 3.30).

## What you own

- Advice on personal data in AI use, including DPIAs.
- Personal data breach handling.
- The rules on what staff may put into Copilot.

## What AI can do for you

- A first read of a DPIA or supplier document.
- Compare a process with a policy.

## What you must not hand over

- Your independent judgement on whether processing is lawful.
- Deciding on breach notifications.

## Your tools

- The Laya Privacy & AI Check: try it, and correct it. It sends people to you, so it should match how you work.
- [Data protection](../../best-practices/06-data-protection.md).

## The evidence you leave

- DPIAs and advice given.
- The breach register.

## Watch out for

- The Irish DPC expects a formal risk assessment, data rights processes and a retention schedule for AI (July 2024 guidance).
