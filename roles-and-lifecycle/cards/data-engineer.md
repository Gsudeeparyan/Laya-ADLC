# Data engineer

Whoever changes Snowflake, Oracle or data pipelines.

## What you own

- Data staying correct under retries, replays and change.
- Member data staying masked and minimal.
- Migrations with a way back.

## What AI can do for you

- Write and explain SQL and PL/SQL.
- Draft pipelines and migrations.
- Generate synthetic test data.

## What you must not hand over

- Running anything against production.
- Access to real member data: agents work with synthetic data.
- Destructive changes, which a person reviews and runs.

## Your tools

- `data-design`, `member-data-safety`, `legacy-change`.
- Laya Data Engineer.

## The evidence you leave

- Reconciliations that balance, and rollback plans.
