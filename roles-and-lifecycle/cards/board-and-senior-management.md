# Board and senior management

EIOPA calls this the administrative, management or supervisory body. It's responsible for the overall use of AI in the organisation (paragraph 3.30).

## What you own

- The vision and policy for how Laya develops and uses AI, and telling people about it.
- Enough knowledge of how AI is used here and of its risks.
- Risk appetite for AI.
- Making sure roles and responsibilities are clear.

## What AI can do for you

- Summarise papers and registers for you.
- Help you prepare questions.

## What you must not hand over

- Overall responsibility. It can't be delegated to a vendor or a tool: firms stay responsible for AI they use, whether built or bought (paragraph 3.11).

## Your tools

- The AI use register, in summary.
- [Regulation](../../best-practices/07-regulation.md) and [measuring value](../../best-practices/08-measuring-value.md).

## The evidence you leave

- An approved AI policy, and minutes showing it was discussed.
- Evidence of training for board members.

## Watch out for

- Knowledge concentrated in one or two people. The Central Bank has raised this kind of key-person risk in other areas.
