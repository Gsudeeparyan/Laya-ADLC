# Vendor owner

Whoever manages Microsoft, GitHub and other AI suppliers. Firms stay responsible for third-party AI, and need enough information and assurance from suppliers (EIOPA paragraph 3.11).

## What you own

- Contracts, data processing terms and exit plans.
- The ICT third-party register, where it applies.
- Tracking what suppliers change: models, terms, where data goes.

## What AI can do for you

- Compare contract versions, and draft supplier questionnaires.

## What you must not hand over

- Interpreting a contract: that's for Legal.
- Approving a new AI supplier without the DPO and Compliance.

## Your tools

- Laya Document Summariser, Laya Document Co-author (questionnaire mode).

## The evidence you leave

- Supplier assessments, and the third-party register.
