# AI champion

The person in a team who is a bit ahead, and helps the others. You don't need to be technical.

## What you own

- Helping colleagues get started.
- Running team sessions.
- Sharing what works, and passing on what doesn't.

## What AI can do for you

- Prepare sessions and examples.
- Improve the team's prompts.

## What you must not hand over

- Approving what's allowed: that's for the DPO, Compliance and the AI owner.
- Checking everyone's work: each person stays responsible for their own.

## Your tools

- [Being an AI champion](../../non-technical/training/being-an-ai-champion.md).
- The [team session plan](../../non-technical/training/run-a-team-session.md).
- Laya Prompt Coach.

## The evidence you leave

- A short monthly note to the library owner, with no names.
