# AI owner (AI officer or committee)

EIOPA suggests an AI officer who provides oversight and advice, or an AI or data committee (paragraph 3.31). It's Laya's choice whether to have one. Somebody must do the job **[to confirm]**.

## What you own

- The AI use register.
- Advising on and deciding about T3 uses.
- Coordinating the DPO, Compliance, Risk, IT and the business.
- Reviewing the approach regularly, as the number and importance of AI uses change.

## What AI can do for you

- Keep the register tidy, and flag overdue reviews.
- Draft policy wording and reports.

## What you must not hand over

- Decisions on what may go ahead.
- Independence: the AI owner shouldn't approve their own projects.

## Your tools

- The register, the [risk tiers](../risk-tiers.md) and the Laya Privacy & AI Check.

## The evidence you leave

- The register, decisions and their reasons.
