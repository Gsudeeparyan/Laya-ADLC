# Internal audit

Independent assurance over how AI is governed.

## What you own

- Testing whether the controls here work in practice.
- Reporting independently.

## What AI can do for you

- Sample registers and logs, and compare them.
- Summarise evidence.

## What you must not hand over

- Your conclusions.
- Independence: you test these controls, and you don't design them.

## Your tools

- The register, role cards, pull request history, audit logs and change records.

## The evidence you leave

- Audit reports and findings.
