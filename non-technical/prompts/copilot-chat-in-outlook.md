# Copilot Chat in Outlook

**This works on the free version too.** Copilot Chat in Outlook can read your own email, calendar, meetings and chats, plus a limited set of your files, such as your OneDrive and files shared directly with you. Open it from the Copilot button in Outlook.

It only sees what you can already see. It's the easiest way for someone on the free version to use Copilot on their own work.

## Your inbox

> What needs a reply from me today? Put the most urgent first.

> Summarise the emails about [topic] from the last two weeks. What was decided, and what's still open?

> Which emails from this week have a question for me that I haven't answered?

> I've been away since [date]. What did I miss that I need to act on?

## Your day

> What meetings do I have today, and what should I read before each one?

> I'm meeting [name] tomorrow. What have we emailed about recently, and what's still open?

> Which of my meetings next week could be an email? Tell me why you think so.

## Writing

> Draft a reply to this email saying [what]. Keep it short and friendly, with the main point first.

> Make this draft clearer and more polite, without changing what it asks for.

> Turn this email thread into a three-line update for my manager.

## Before you rely on it

- **Open the original** before acting on anything important. A summary can miss things.
- **Read drafts before you send them.** Copilot writes; you send.
- **Emails can be wrong, old or even hostile.** If Copilot acts strangely after reading an email (asks for passwords, tells you to click a link), stop and tell the IT service desk.
- **Keep member details out of what you ask.** "The complaint from last Tuesday" works as well as a name.
