# HR and people

**Where AI helps:** drafting policies, job descriptions and announcements, and answering "what does our policy say?".

**What stays with you:** every decision about a person. EU law treats AI used in hiring, promotion and evaluating staff as high-risk, so these stay with people.

## Jobs AI can help with

These work on the free version as well as the paid licence. Change the [brackets], and leave real names and member details out.

| Your job | Use | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| Draft a job description | Laya Document Co-author | Draft a job description for [role] from these notes. Use plain, inclusive language, and only list requirements the job truly needs. |
| Check wording for bias | Laya Comms Writer | Check this job advert for wording that could put people off, and for jargon. Suggest changes. |
| Summarise a policy | Laya Document Summariser (upload it) | Summarise this policy for line managers: what they must do, when, and who to ask. |
| Write an announcement | Laya Comms Writer | Draft an all-staff note about [change]: what's changing, when, why, and what people need to do. |
| Plan an onboarding | Laya Project Planner | Build a first-90-days plan for a new [role], with an owner for each step. |
| See survey themes | Laya PII Guard, then Laya Excel Insights | With names removed: what are the main themes in these survey comments? Leave out any group smaller than 5, and any comment that identifies someone. |
| Write a training plan | Laya Presentation Builder | Help me build a short session on [topic] for new managers. |

## If you have the paid licence

- **Laya Policy Finder:** give managers the wording of a policy, with a link.
- **Copilot in Word:** a first draft of a policy, from your notes and the old version.

## Check before you use it

- Check every legal point with your HR or legal adviser. Employment law changes, and AI may be out of date.
- Read drafts for fairness and for what they imply about anyone.
- Check that anonymised results can't identify a person. In a small team, even a role or a comment can do it.

## Never hand these to AI

- Screening, ranking or scoring CVs and candidates.
- Judging performance, deciding on pay, promotion, discipline or dismissal.
- Monitoring staff, or analysing how someone writes, speaks or seems to feel.
- Advice on a real case, without an HR professional.

## Data to watch

HR files often hold health information, disciplinary records and family details. Keep them out of chats. For any use of AI with staff data, use the Laya Privacy & AI Check first. Staff should be told how AI is used in decisions that affect them.

## When you're unsure

Use the **Laya Privacy & AI Check**, or ask your manager. If something has already gone wrong, see [When something goes wrong](../staff-guide/when-something-goes-wrong.md).
