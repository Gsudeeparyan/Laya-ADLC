# Office and admin support

**Where AI helps:** managing the flow: minutes, letters, diaries, packs and trackers.

**What stays with you:** what goes out in someone else's name, and your discretion with what you see.

## Jobs AI can help with

These work on the free version as well as the paid licence. Change the [brackets], and leave real names and member details out.

| Your job | Use | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| Write minutes | Laya Meeting Actions | Here are my notes from [meeting]. Write the minutes: decisions, actions with owners and dates, and open points. |
| Draft correspondence | Laya Comms Writer | Draft a reply from [role] saying [what]. Formal but warm, under 150 words. |
| Sort your inbox | Copilot Chat in Outlook | What needs a reply from me today? Put the most urgent first. |
| Prepare a meeting pack | Laya Document Summariser (upload the papers) | Summarise each paper in two lines for [role], with the decision each one asks for. |
| Keep a tracker | Laya Excel Builder | Make me a simple tracker for [what], with an owner, a due date and a status. |
| Tidy a list | Laya Excel Data Cleaner | Tidy this list: remove duplicates, fix the name and date formats, and tell me exactly what you changed. |
| Make slides presentable | Laya Presentation Builder | Turn these notes into a clean short deck, and make each title say the point of the slide. |

## If you have the paid licence

- **Laya Meeting Recap:** minutes straight from a transcribed Teams meeting.
- **Copilot in Outlook:** summarise long threads, and coach you on tone.
- **Laya Catch Me Up:** a quick start to the day.

## Check before you use it

- Read anything that goes out in someone else's name, and have them approve it as usual.
- Check names, dates, titles and room details.
- Check minutes against what was really decided, not just discussed.

## Never hand these to AI

- Sending anything on someone's behalf without their approval.
- Using a manager's access to look up things you wouldn't otherwise see.
- Recording or transcribing meetings without following Laya's rules.

## Data to watch

You may see confidential papers and personal matters. Don't put HR, board or legal material into a chat unless the owner has agreed.

## When you're unsure

Use the **Laya Privacy & AI Check**, or ask your manager. If something has already gone wrong, see [When something goes wrong](../staff-guide/when-something-goes-wrong.md).
