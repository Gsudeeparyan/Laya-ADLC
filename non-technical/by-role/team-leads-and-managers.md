# Team leads and managers

**Where AI helps:** planning, writing and catching up, like everyone else. You also set the tone for how your team uses AI.

**What stays with you:** the judgement of your people, and every decision about them. You also stay accountable for your team's work, whoever or whatever drafted it.

## Jobs AI can help with

These work on the free version as well as the paid licence. Change the [brackets], and leave real names and member details out.

| Your job | Use | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| Agree how your team uses AI | Laya Document Co-author | Help me write a one-page note for my team: what we use AI for, what we don't, and how we check it. We work on [area]. |
| Spot where AI could help | Laya Idea Refiner | My team spends a lot of time on [task]. Help me work out whether AI could help, and what could go wrong. |
| Prepare a difficult message | Copilot Chat | I need to tell my team that [general situation]. Help me say it clearly and kindly. |
| Write a status update | Laya Project Planner | Turn these notes into a short honest update for [who]. |
| Run a team session | Laya Presentation Builder | Help me build a 30-minute session on using Copilot safely for a team of [number]. |
| Screen a new use | Laya Privacy & AI Check | My team wants to use AI for [what]. Is that OK? |

## If you have the paid licence

- **Laya Catch Me Up:** the start of your day or week.
- **Laya Meeting Recap:** actions from team meetings.
- **Copilot in Word and Outlook:** first drafts of your own writing.

## Check before you use it

- When someone hands you AI-assisted work, ask how they checked it. Don't ask them to hide that they used it.
- Keep the same standard as for any other work, and spot-check at first.
- Look at where the time saved is going: is it going into checking, or into doing more?

## Never hand these to AI

- Writing performance reviews, or deciding on someone's pay or prospects.
- Monitoring staff, or analysing their messages, tone or activity.
- Deciding who gets a licence based on anything other than the job.

## Data to watch

Be careful with what you ask about your team. "Summarise what [name] has been doing" isn't an acceptable use of Copilot, even if your account can reach it.

## Five things your team needs from you

1. **Say it's OK.** Teams with a clear position on AI use it better and more safely. Unclear teams either avoid it or hide it.
2. **Make it safe to own up.** If someone reports a mistake quickly, thank them. That's how you find out early.
3. **Give people time to learn.** Try the [30-minute team session](../training/run-a-team-session.md), and make sure everyone does the [AI basics](../training/ai-basics.md). Laya has a duty to support staff in understanding AI.
4. **Share what works.** Ten minutes at a team meeting: one prompt that worked, one that didn't.
5. **Know who has what.** Some of your team may have the free version and some the paid licence. Don't build a process only half the team can follow. Point out the free route.

## Questions to ask

- Who's accountable for this piece of work? (It should be a person.)
- What did you check it against?
- Does it use anyone's personal data? Could it work without it?
- Is this in the [AI use register](../../roles-and-lifecycle/README.md#the-ai-use-register)? Anything beyond personal drafting should be.

## When you're unsure

Use the **Laya Privacy & AI Check**, or ask your manager. If something has already gone wrong, see [When something goes wrong](../staff-guide/when-something-goes-wrong.md).
