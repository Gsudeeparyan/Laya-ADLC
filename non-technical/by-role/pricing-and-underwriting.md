# Pricing and underwriting

**Where AI helps:** writing up methods, explaining workbooks, cleaning data and preparing papers. This is the area of Laya where AI needs the most care.

**What stays with you:** every pricing and risk decision, and the models behind them. EU law treats AI used for risk assessment and pricing in health insurance as high-risk.

## Jobs AI can help with

These work on the free version as well as the paid licence. Change the [brackets], and leave real names and member details out.

| Your job | Use | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| Explain an inherited workbook | Laya Excel Explainer | What does this workbook do? Walk me through the sheets and formulas, and flag anything that looks fragile. |
| Document a method | Laya Document Co-author | Help me write up this method so a colleague could follow and challenge it. I'll explain the steps. Mark any assumption I haven't justified as [to confirm]. |
| Clean a data extract | Laya Excel Data Cleaner | Clean this extract and report exactly what changed, with row counts before and after. Don't remove outliers without asking me. |
| Check a calculation | Laya Second Check | Recalculate these figures and tell me any that don't match. Show the working. |
| Prepare a paper | Laya Document Co-author | Draft the paper for [committee] from these notes. Keep my numbers as they are, and mark anything unsupported. |
| Track model changes | Laya Excel Builder | Make me a register of model changes, with the date, the change, the reason, who made it and who reviewed it. |

## If you have the paid licence

- **Copilot in Excel:** explain a formula, or take a first look at aggregated data.
- **Analyst:** work through aggregated data with its steps shown, so you can check them. Never use it for decisions about individuals.

## Check before you use it

- Every number that matters is reproduced by a person, in the original model.
- Keep a record of what AI helped with on anything that feeds a decision.
- Check for proxy effects: a field that seems harmless can stand in for a protected characteristic.

## Never hand these to AI

- Risk assessment or pricing for individuals. **Stop and speak to the AI owner, Compliance and the DPO first.** This is high-risk under the EU AI Act (Annex III, point 5(c)).
- Choosing rating factors, or setting assumptions, by itself.
- Producing figures for reserving, solvency or regulatory returns without independent checking by the actuarial function.
- Using member health data in a chat.

## Data to watch

Assume everything in this area is sensitive. Work with aggregated data wherever you can. Ask the DPO before any new use of member-level data with AI.

## When you're unsure

Use the **Laya Privacy & AI Check**, or ask your manager. If something has already gone wrong, see [When something goes wrong](../staff-guide/when-something-goes-wrong.md).
