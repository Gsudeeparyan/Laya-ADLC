# Finance

**Where AI helps:** reconciling, explaining variances, tidying exports and turning numbers into plain-English commentary.

**What stays with you:** the numbers themselves, and the sign-off. AI can make arithmetic mistakes and write them confidently.

## Jobs AI can help with

These work on the free version as well as the paid licence. Change the [brackets], and leave real names and member details out.

| Your job | Use | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| Reconcile two lists | Laya Excel Data Cleaner | Match these two files on [reference]. Show what's only in one, what's different, and the totals on each side. |
| Explain a variance | Laya Excel Insights | Here are budget and actual by cost centre. Which movements are biggest, and what do they add up to? I'll add the reasons. |
| Write commentary | Laya Comms Writer | Turn these figures and my reasons into five plain-English lines for the management pack. Keep every number as it is. |
| Fix a formula | Laya Excel Expert | This total should be [amount] but shows [amount]. Find the cause in this workbook and show me before you change anything. |
| Explain a workbook | Laya Excel Explainer | I've inherited this workbook. What does each sheet do, and where do the numbers come from? |
| Recheck the numbers | Laya Second Check | Recalculate every total and percentage in this report against the data I've uploaded. |
| Write down a process | Laya Process & SOP Builder | Help me document the month-end steps for [process], including who checks what. |

## If you have the paid licence

- **Copilot in Excel:** quick pivots and formulas.
- **Analyst:** find what's driving a change, with its steps shown.
- **Laya Catch Me Up:** see what's outstanding during month-end.

## Check before you use it

- Tie every total back to the ledger or source system. AI's total isn't a control.
- Keep your normal review and segregation of duties. AI doesn't count as the second reviewer.
- Check for hard-coded numbers in place of formulas.

## Never hand these to AI

- Posting, approving or releasing anything.
- Signing off accounts, regulatory returns or tax positions.
- Payment decisions, or anything that changes bank details.
- Accounting judgements, without a qualified person reviewing them.

## Data to watch

Bank details, payroll and unpublished results. Keep them out of chats unless the job needs them, and clean files first. Be alert to emails asking to change payment details: AI makes these scams more convincing.
