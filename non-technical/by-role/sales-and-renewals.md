# Sales and renewals, including group schemes

**Where AI helps:** preparing proposals, tidying pipelines, following up after meetings, and turning product documents into clear summaries.

**What stays with you:** advice on which plan suits someone, prices, and anything a customer relies on when buying. Sales conduct is regulated: AI prepares, you advise.

## Jobs AI can help with

These work on the free version as well as the paid licence. Change the [brackets], and leave real names and member details out.

| Your job | Use | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| Prepare for a client meeting | Copilot Chat | I'm meeting a [size, sector] company about their group scheme. What questions should I ask, and what might they be worried about? |
| Draft a proposal | Laya Document Co-author | Draft a proposal for [type of client] from this approved product document. Mark any price, benefit or date I need to add as [to confirm]. |
| Summarise plan documents | Laya Document Summariser (upload them) | Summarise the main differences between these plan documents in a table. Quote the wording for each. |
| Write a renewal email | Laya Comms Writer | Draft a friendly renewal reminder that asks them to [action] by [date]. Plain English, under 120 words. I'll check it against the approved wording. |
| Tidy a pipeline spreadsheet | Laya Excel Expert | Add a dropdown for stage, and highlight anything with no contact in 30 days. Show me what you'll change first. |
| Follow up after a meeting | Laya Meeting Actions | Here are my notes from the meeting. What did we agree, who's doing what, and write the follow-up email. |
| Build a pitch | Laya Presentation Builder | Help me build a short deck for [client type]. Here's the story I want to tell, and here's the approved product content. |

## If you have the paid licence

- **Laya Catch Me Up:** before a renewal call, see what's open with that client.
- **Copilot in PowerPoint:** a first deck from your Word proposal.
- **Laya Policy Finder:** check what the sales guidance says.

## Check before you use it

- Every benefit, price, waiting period and date comes from a current approved document, not from AI.
- Have Compliance's approved wording to hand for anything about products. Run customer-facing material through your normal approval.
- Check any statement about a competitor against something you can show. AI's knowledge of competitors may be out of date or wrong.

## Never hand these to AI

- Recommending which plan a customer should buy.
- Quoting prices or working out what someone will pay.
- Assessing a person's health or risk to decide what to offer them. AI used for risk assessment and pricing in health insurance is high-risk under the EU AI Act.
- Sending anything to a customer that you haven't read.

## Data to watch

Employee lists from group schemes hold many people's personal data. Keep them out of chats unless the job needs them, and clean them first with the Laya PII Guard.

Use the **Laya Privacy & AI Check**, or ask your manager. If something has already gone wrong, see [When something goes wrong](../staff-guide/when-something-goes-wrong.md).
