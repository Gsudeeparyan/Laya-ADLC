# Claims

**Where AI helps:** understanding procedures, writing clearly, cleaning data and spotting patterns in anonymised figures.

**What stays with you:** every decision on a claim. Whether a claim is paid, part-paid or declined is decided by a person, with the reason recorded.

## Jobs AI can help with

These work on the free version as well as the paid licence. Change the [brackets], and leave real names and member details out.

| Your job | Use | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| Understand a procedure | Laya Document Summariser (upload it) | Summarise the steps in this procedure, and list every deadline and every point where a person must approve something. |
| Write a clear letter | Laya Comms Writer | Rewrite this letter in plain English for a member, keeping every fact, amount and date the same. Use our approved wording for [reason] where I've marked it. |
| Clean up an export | Laya PII Guard, then Laya Excel Data Cleaner | This export has duplicates and mixed date formats. Clean it and tell me exactly what you changed, with row counts before and after. |
| See where the backlog is | Laya PII Guard, then Laya Excel Insights | With personal data removed: how many claims are open by age band and by type? What's changed since last month? |
| Fix a wrong total | Laya Excel Expert | The total in [cell] should be about [amount] but shows [amount]. Find out why before you change anything. |
| Improve a process | Laya Process & SOP Builder | Help me map how we handle [type of claim] now, and show me where it waits, gets handed over or gets re-keyed. |
| Track actions from a review | Laya Excel Builder | Make me a tracker for actions from our claims review, with an owner, a due date and a status. |

## If you have the paid licence

- **Laya Policy Finder:** find what the procedure or benefit document says, with a link.
- **Copilot in Excel:** quick pivots on figures with personal data removed.
- **Laya Meeting Recap:** actions from the weekly claims review.

## Check before you use it

- Recheck every total from AI against the source. Row counts must reconcile: original = clean + removed.
- Compare like with like. A busy month may just be a timing difference.
- Don't report on groups smaller than 5 people.

## Never hand these to AI

- Deciding, scoring or ranking a claim.
- Judging medical need, or whether a treatment was appropriate.
- Deciding whether someone is committing fraud. AI can't accuse anyone: suspicions go through the proper route.
- Anything a member will read, without a person checking it first.

## Data to watch

Claims data is health data, the most sensitive kind Laya holds. Keep diagnosis, treatment, provider and invoice details out of chats. If the job needs a file, clean it first with the Laya PII Guard, and keep the clean copy in your team's restricted folder.
