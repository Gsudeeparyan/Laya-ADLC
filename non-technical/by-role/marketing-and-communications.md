# Marketing and communications

**Where AI helps:** first drafts, plain-English rewrites, variations for different channels, and turning long documents into short messages.

**What stays with you:** what Laya says in public. Every claim needs to be true, approved and fair. AI can produce polished copy that's wrong.

## Jobs AI can help with

These work on the free version as well as the paid licence. Change the [brackets], and leave real names and member details out.

| Your job | Use | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| Draft a campaign brief | Laya Document Co-author | Help me write a campaign brief: the audience, the one message, what we want them to do, and how we'll measure it. |
| Rewrite in plain English | Laya Comms Writer | Rewrite this for members in plain English. Keep every benefit and condition exactly as it is, and tell me anything you weren't sure of. |
| Write variations | Copilot Chat | Give me five subject lines and three short social posts from this approved message. Stick to what it says. Add no claims. |
| Turn a report into a story | Laya Presentation Builder | Help me turn this report into a short deck for [audience]: the headline of each slide should tell the story. |
| Sum up feedback | Laya PII Guard, then Laya Excel Insights | With names removed: what are the main themes in these comments, and how many mention each? |
| Plan a launch | Laya Project Planner | Build a launch plan for [campaign] with a timeline, owners and risks, working back from [date]. |

## If you have the paid licence

- **Copilot in Word and PowerPoint:** first drafts from your own approved material.
- **Researcher:** background on a topic, with sources. Check them yourself, and keep public and internal sources apart.

## Check before you use it

- Every product claim comes from an approved source, and goes through Compliance as normal.
- Check statistics, quotes and sources by finding the original. AI invents convincing ones.
- Check brand and tone yourself, and read it as a member would. For health topics, avoid anything that sounds like medical advice.
- Check any image: AI can make people and details look wrong, and rights may be unclear.

## Never hand these to AI

- Publishing anything you haven't read and had approved.
- Inventing member stories, testimonials, statistics or quotes.
- Targeting people based on their health, or using member data for marketing without checking the permissions.
- Comparing Laya with competitors, without evidence you can show.

## Data to watch

Member stories and photos need consent. Marketing lists hold personal data, so keep them out of chats. Tell people when something they're dealing with is AI, where the law or Laya's policy says to **[Compliance to confirm]**.

## When you're unsure

Use the **Laya Privacy & AI Check**, or ask your manager. If something has already gone wrong, see [When something goes wrong](../staff-guide/when-something-goes-wrong.md).
