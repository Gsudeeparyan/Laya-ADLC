# Compliance, risk and legal

**Where AI helps:** reading long regulations, comparing documents, drafting from your own notes, and keeping registers.

**What stays with you:** every interpretation and judgement. AI invents citations that look real, so nothing goes forward unchecked.

## Jobs AI can help with

These work on the free version as well as the paid licence. Change the [brackets], and leave real names and member details out.

| Your job | Use | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| Summarise a regulation | Laya Document Summariser (upload the official text) | Summarise this for a non-lawyer: who it applies to, what they must do, by when. Quote the article for each. Use only this document. |
| Compare with a policy | Laya Document Summariser (upload both) | Compare this policy with this regulation. For each requirement: covered, partly covered or not covered, with the wording from each. |
| Compare two versions | Laya Document Summariser (upload both) | What changed between these versions? Flag changes in strength, like "should" to "must". |
| Draft from your notes | Laya Document Co-author | Draft a board paper from these notes. Keep my conclusions as I wrote them, and mark anything unsupported as [to confirm]. |
| Keep a register | Laya Excel Builder | Build a register for [obligations / risks / AI uses], with an owner, a status and a review date. |
| Write a questionnaire | Laya Document Co-author | Write a questionnaire for a supplier about how they handle our data. I'll tell you what we need back. |
| Screen a new use | Laya Privacy & AI Check | Someone wants to use AI for [what]. Take me through the screening. |

## If you have the paid licence

- **Laya Policy Finder:** find what Laya's own policies say.
- **Researcher:** background reading, with sources. Treat it as a list of leads to verify at the original.
- **Copilot in Word:** ask questions of a long document you have open.

## Check before you use it

- **Open every citation at its source.** Article numbers, case names and dates are what AI gets wrong most convincingly.
- Check that you have the current text. Regulations are amended, and AI's knowledge may be older.
- Note when AI helped with something that goes to a regulator, in line with your own rules.

## Never hand these to AI

- Legal conclusions, or a view on whether Laya is compliant.
- Anything sent to a regulator without a named person checking it line by line.
- Deciding complaints or breach notifications.
- Privileged or highly confidential matters, unless your team has agreed how.

## Data to watch

Regulatory correspondence, complaints and investigations. Ask whether legal privilege could be affected before you put privileged material into any tool.

## When you're unsure

Use the **Laya Privacy & AI Check**, or ask your manager. If something has already gone wrong, see [When something goes wrong](../staff-guide/when-something-goes-wrong.md).
