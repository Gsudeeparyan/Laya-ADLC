# Member services and the contact centre

**Where AI helps:** preparing, writing and finding things, so you have more time for the member in front of you.

**What stays with you:** what you tell a member about their cover or claim, and how you treat someone who's upset or vulnerable. AI helps you prepare. You talk to the member.

## Jobs AI can help with

These work on the free version as well as the paid licence. Change the [brackets], and leave real names and member details out.

| Your job | Use | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| Reply to a member's email | Laya Comms Writer | Help me reply to a member who asked [general question]. Use plain English and a warm tone, with the answer first. I'll add the details myself. |
| Explain something complicated | Laya Document Summariser (upload the document) | Explain what this section means in plain words, as I'd say it on a call. Quote the wording you relied on. |
| Find what changed in a booklet | Laya Document Summariser (upload both) | What changed between these two versions? Flag anything that changes what a member needs to do. |
| Spot what members keep asking | Laya PII Guard, then Laya Excel Insights | Here's an export of call reasons with personal data removed. What are the top five reasons this month compared with last, and what's changed? |
| Write a how-to for the team | Laya Process & SOP Builder | Help me write a simple step-by-step guide for handling [type of query]. I'll tell you how we do it now. |
| Draft a script or FAQ | Laya Document Co-author | Draft an FAQ for colleagues on [topic], from this approved document. Mark anything it doesn't answer as [to confirm]. |
| Check a letter before it goes | Laya Second Check | This is going to a member. Check it against the source and tell me anything I can't stand over. |

## If you have the paid licence

- **Laya Policy Finder:** find the wording in the approved documents while you're on a case, with a link to check.
- **Copilot in Outlook:** summarise a long thread with a member's broker or a colleague before you reply.
- **Laya Catch Me Up:** after leave, see what's waiting on you, with links.

## Check before you use it

- Check every statement about cover against the member's actual plan and the current documents. AI can't see which plan someone is on unless you show it, and may guess.
- Check dates, amounts and waiting periods one by one.
- Use approved wording where your team has it.

## Never hand these to AI

- Telling a member whether they're covered, or how much they'll get.
- Deciding a complaint, or how to treat a vulnerable member.
- Giving medical advice, or suggesting what treatment someone should have.
- Replying to a member without you reading it first.

## Data to watch

Member names, policy numbers, health details and call notes. You rarely need them to get help with wording. Say "a member with a query about [topic]".

## When you're unsure

Use the **Laya Privacy & AI Check**, or ask your manager. If something has already gone wrong, see [When something goes wrong](../staff-guide/when-something-goes-wrong.md).
