## Your team isn't listed, or works differently?

These playbooks are starting points, written from how health insurers generally work. They don't describe Laya's own procedures, which your manager and your procedures take precedence over.

The way to use one: pick the job closest to yours, and try the prompt with something real but low-risk. Then tell your AI champion what worked, so the playbook gets better. To make your own, ask the **Laya Document Co-author** to write one in the same layout.

## Every playbook has the same five parts

1. **Where AI helps, and what stays with you.**
2. **Jobs AI can help with:** a table of the job, the agent and the words to use, on free or paid.
3. **If you have the paid licence:** what's extra.
4. **Check before you use it:** what goes wrong in this kind of work.
5. **Never hand these to AI:** the decisions that stay with people.
