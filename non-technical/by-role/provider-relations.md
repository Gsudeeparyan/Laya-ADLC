# Provider relations

**Where AI helps:** keeping on top of correspondence, summarising long agreements for your own understanding, and tracking what's open with each hospital or consultant.

**What stays with you:** negotiating positions, what a contract means, and relationships. AI helps you prepare, and Legal interprets contracts.

## Jobs AI can help with

These work on the free version as well as the paid licence. Change the [brackets], and leave real names and member details out.

| Your job | Use | Try saying |
|---|---|---|
| Summarise an agreement | Laya Document Summariser (upload it) | Summarise this agreement: the term, the main obligations, the dates, the notice periods. Quote each one with its clause number. |
| Compare two versions | Laya Document Summariser (upload both) | What changed between these versions? Flag changes of meaning, such as a new obligation, date or amount. |
| Write to a provider | Laya Comms Writer | Draft a clear, courteous letter telling a provider about [change] from [date], and what they need to do. |
| Track open items | Laya Excel Builder | Make me a tracker for provider queries, with the provider, the topic, the owner, the status and the date raised. |
| See patterns in figures | Laya Excel Insights | With personal data removed: how has activity changed by provider type this quarter against last? What should I look into? |
| Prepare for a meeting | Laya Document Co-author | Help me write a briefing note for a meeting with [provider type]: what we want, what they'll want, and what's open. |

## If you have the paid licence

- **Laya Catch Me Up:** everything open with one provider before you meet them.
- **Laya Meeting Recap:** decisions and actions from a provider meeting.
- **Copilot in Word:** a first draft of correspondence, from your own notes.

## Check before you use it

- Check every clause reference, date and rate against the signed agreement.
- A summary of a contract isn't advice. For what it means, ask Legal.
- Check that figures compare like with like, such as the same period and the same type of activity.

## Never hand these to AI

- Interpreting a contract, or saying what Laya is legally bound to do.
- Setting rates or negotiating positions.
- Judging a clinician's practice or the quality of care.
- Sending commercially sensitive terms to anyone without the normal approval.

## Data to watch

Rates and contract terms are commercially sensitive. Patient details on invoices are health data, so keep them out of chats.
