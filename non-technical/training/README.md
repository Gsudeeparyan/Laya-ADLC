# AI training for everyone at Laya

No technical background is needed. EU law expects Laya to support its staff in understanding the AI they use, and this is a practical way to start.

| Material | For | Time |
|---|---|---|
| [AI basics](ai-basics.md) | Everyone | 20 minutes to read |
| [Quick quiz](quiz.md) | Everyone, after the basics | 5 minutes |
| [One-page rules](one-page-rules.md) | Everyone: print it or pin it | 1 minute |
| [Run a team session](run-a-team-session.md) | Team leads and AI champions | 30 minutes with your team |
| [Being an AI champion](being-an-ai-champion.md) | People who volunteer to help their team | About an hour a week |

Word versions are in `ready-to-use/`. Keep a note of who has done the basics, in line with how Laya records training **[to confirm with HR and Compliance]**.
