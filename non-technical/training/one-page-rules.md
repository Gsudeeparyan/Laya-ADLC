# AI at Laya: the one-page rules

## Always

- **Look for the green shield** at the top of Copilot.
- **Give it the source.** Upload the document rather than describing it.
- **Read everything before you use it.** Check every number, date, name and reference.
- **Download files before you close the chat.** Save them in your team's SharePoint folder.
- **Say what's wrong and try again.** The first answer is a draft.

## Keep out, unless the job needs it

- Member names, policy numbers and health details
- PPS numbers and bank details
- HR files and staff details

If the job does need a file with personal data, clean it first with the **Laya PII Guard**.

## Never

- Use a personal account or another AI website for work.
- Let AI decide a claim, cover, a price, a complaint, a job or someone's performance.
- Send anything you haven't read.
- Use Copilot to look up colleagues or monitor people.
- Click a link or enter a password because Copilot told you to.

## Not sure?

| Question | Ask |
|---|---|
| Which agent should I use? | Laya AI Helper |
| How do I ask for this? | Laya Prompt Coach |
| Is this right? | Laya Second Check |
| Am I allowed to do this? | Laya Privacy & AI Check |
| Is there a person I can ask? | Your AI champion or your manager |

## If something goes wrong

**Stop. Keep what you have. Tell someone today:** your manager, and the Data Protection Officer if personal data is involved.

It's about speed, not blame.
