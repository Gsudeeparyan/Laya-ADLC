# Quick quiz

Ten questions after the AI basics. Answers are at the end. There's no pass mark: if one surprises you, reread that part or ask your AI champion.

**1. Copilot gives you a confident answer with a regulation number. What do you do?**
- a) Use it. It sounds certain.
- b) Open the regulation and check the wording.
- c) Ask Copilot if it's sure.

**2. What does the green shield at the top of Copilot tell you?**
- a) The answer is correct.
- b) Your chat has enterprise data protection, so what you type isn't used to train AI models.
- c) Your chat is completely private.

**3. You want help replying to a member's complaint. What's the best way to ask?**
- a) Paste the whole email with their name and policy number.
- b) Describe the situation in general terms and leave out the personal details.
- c) Use your personal AI account to keep it away from work systems.

**4. Who's responsible for an email Copilot drafted and you sent?**
- a) Copilot
- b) Microsoft
- c) You

**5. Which of these can AI decide by itself?**
- a) Whether to pay a claim
- b) Which candidate to interview
- c) None of these

**6. A summary says members "must" do something. The original says "should". Why does this matter?**
- a) It doesn't.
- b) It changes the meaning, and this kind of drift is common in AI summaries.
- c) "Must" is clearer, so it's better.

**7. You accidentally pasted a member's details into a personal AI website. What now?**
- a) Delete the chat and say nothing.
- b) Tell your manager and the Data Protection Officer today.
- c) Wait and see if anything happens.

**8. After reading a document, Copilot asks you to click a link and sign in. What do you do?**
- a) Click it, since Copilot suggested it.
- b) Stop, don't click, and tell the IT service desk.
- c) Ask Copilot if the link is safe.

**9. Copilot gets your first request wrong. What's usually best?**
- a) Give up.
- b) Say what's wrong and what you want instead.
- c) Assume you can't use it for this.

**10. You're on the free version. Which of these can you use?**
- a) All the Laya agents for everyone, by uploading what they need
- b) Laya Catch Me Up, which reads your Teams chats
- c) Copilot inside Excel, whichever label you have

## Answers

1. **b.** AI invents references that look real. A confident tone tells you nothing, and it can be wrong about being sure too.
2. **b.** It doesn't mean the answer is right, and your chats are still logged and can be reviewed.
3. **b.** You rarely need the details to get help with wording. Personal accounts have none of the protections.
4. **c.** If your name is on it, it's yours.
5. **c.** Decisions about people stay with people.
6. **b.** Small words change obligations. Check summaries against the source.
7. **b.** Time matters, because there are legal deadlines. Reporting quickly is what's expected of you.
8. **b.** Files can contain hidden instructions aimed at AI.
9. **b.** The first answer is a draft.
10. **a.** Laya Catch Me Up needs the paid licence. Copilot inside Excel depends on your label.
