# AI at work: start here

This folder is for everyone at Laya who doesn't write code. You don't need to be technical, and you don't need to know anything about AI to start.

## Three steps to get going

**1. Find out which Copilot you have.** Sign in to Microsoft 365 and look at the bottom of the home page. A label sits under your name.

| Your label | What you have | Go to |
|---|---|---|
| **Copilot Chat (Basic)** | The free version, which most people have | [Which Copilot do I have?](staff-guide/which-copilot-do-i-have.md) |
| **M365 Copilot (Basic)** | The free version, with some Copilot in Office apps | [Which Copilot do I have?](staff-guide/which-copilot-do-i-have.md) |
| **M365 Copilot (Premium)** | The paid licence | [What the paid licence adds](paid-licence/README.md) |

Every Laya agent marked "everyone" works on all three.

**2. Open your department's playbook.** It shows the jobs AI can help with in your work, what to say, and what must stay with you.

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| [Member services](by-role/member-services.md) | [Claims](by-role/claims.md) | [Sales and renewals](by-role/sales-and-renewals.md) |
| [Provider relations](by-role/provider-relations.md) | [Pricing and underwriting](by-role/pricing-and-underwriting.md) | [Finance](by-role/finance.md) |
| [HR and people](by-role/hr-and-people.md) | [Marketing and communications](by-role/marketing-and-communications.md) | [Compliance, risk and legal](by-role/compliance-risk-and-legal.md) |
| [Projects and operations](by-role/projects-and-operations.md) | [Office and admin support](by-role/office-and-admin-support.md) | [Team leads and managers](by-role/team-leads-and-managers.md) |

**3. Ask the Laya AI Helper.** Not sure where to begin? Open **Laya AI Helper (Start Here)** in Copilot and tell it what you're trying to do. It finds the right agent and writes your first message for you.

## The five rules

1. **You're responsible for what you send.** AI drafts; you decide. Read every result before using it.
2. **Keep personal data out unless the job needs it.** If it does, clean the file first with the **Laya PII Guard**.
3. **Give it the source.** Upload the document rather than describing it. AI guesses when you don't.
4. **People decide about people.** AI never decides a claim, cover, a price, a complaint, a job or anyone's performance.
5. **If something goes wrong, say so straight away.** It's about speed, not blame. See [When something goes wrong](staff-guide/when-something-goes-wrong.md).
