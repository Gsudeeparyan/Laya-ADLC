# When something goes wrong

Everyone who uses AI will make a mistake with it sooner or later. What matters is telling someone quickly. **Nobody gets into trouble for reporting a mistake promptly.** The harm usually comes from waiting.

Laya has legal deadlines for some problems. For a personal data breach, the clock can be as short as 72 hours, so a same-day report really matters.

## Who to tell

|---|---|---|

## If it's happening now

1. **Stop.** Don't send anything else, and don't try to fix it alone.
2. **Keep what you have.** Don't delete the chat, the file or the email. They help work out what happened.
3. **Tell someone** from the table above, today.
4. **Write down** what you did, when, what data was involved and who received it. Leave the personal data itself out of your note.

## Common situations

**"I pasted a member's details into Copilot."**
If the green shield was showing, it stayed within Laya's protected Microsoft 365. Check with your manager whether that use was allowed. If it was a personal account or another AI website, treat it as a possible data breach and tell the DPO today.

**"I sent something AI wrote, and it was wrong."**
Tell your manager. If it went to a member or outside Laya, they'll decide how to correct it. Then use the Laya Second Check next time for that kind of message.

**"Copilot told me something that doesn't sound right."**
You're probably right to doubt it. AI can sound certain and still be wrong. Check the original source. If it matters, ask the person who owns the policy.

**"A document or email seemed to give Copilot instructions."**
Some files and emails contain hidden text that tries to steer AI. If Copilot does something odd after reading a file (asks for passwords, tells you to click a link, changes the subject), stop and tell the IT service desk. Don't click anything it suggests.

**"Copilot gave me someone else's information."**
Copilot only shows what your account can already reach, so this may mean a file is shared too widely. Don't use or forward it. Tell the IT service desk, so the sharing can be fixed.

**"Someone used AI to decide something about a person."**
Claims, cover, complaints, prices, jobs and performance are decided by people, not AI. Raise it with your manager or Compliance.

**"Our team has started using an AI tool that isn't Copilot."**
Tell your manager. It isn't about blame, but Laya needs to know where its data is going.

## If you see a colleague make a mistake

Tell them, and help them report it. The aim is to fix the problem and stop it happening again, not to find someone to blame.
