# Getting started with Laya's AI agents

Laya's AI agents are helpers inside Microsoft Copilot. Each one is good at one kind of job: building a spreadsheet, writing an email, summarising a document, planning a project. You chat with them in plain English. You don't need to be technical.

Most work on the free version of Copilot as well as the paid one. Not sure which you have? See **Which Copilot do I have?**

## Finding an agent

1. Open **Microsoft Copilot** (in Teams, in Outlook, or at m365.cloud.microsoft/chat).
2. On the left, look under **Agents**. Search for **Laya**.
3. Open the agent you need. To keep it handy, select **...** and **Pin**.
4. Click one of the suggested prompts, or type your request.

Not sure which one? Open **Laya AI Helper (Start Here)** and tell it what you're trying to do.

## Five golden rules

1. **You're responsible for what you send.** AI can make mistakes and sound certain. Read every result before you use it. The **Laya Second Check** can help.
2. **Keep personal data out unless the job needs it.** No member health details, PPS numbers or bank details in a chat just because they were in the file. If the job needs that data, clean it first with the **Laya PII Guard**.
3. **Upload the file, don't describe it.** Use the paperclip or **+** in the chat box. AI guesses when you don't give it the source.
4. **People decide about people.** AI never decides a claim, cover, a price, a complaint, a job or someone's performance.
5. **Download files before you close the chat.** Files the agent gives you are only kept during the chat. Save them in your team's SharePoint folder.

If something goes wrong, tell someone the same day. See **When something goes wrong**.

## How it works, step by step

1. Tell the agent what you want, in your own words.
2. It asks a few questions at a time (never more than 3), each with a **suggested answer**. Reply **yes** to accept them all, or just tell it which ones to change.
3. If your request is clear, it just does it and shows you exactly what changed. You always get a new copy, so nothing is lost. Before it deletes or overwrites anything, or if your request could mean two things, it asks you first.
4. It gives you the result. Download any file and save it in SharePoint.
5. Every reply ends with **What next?** and a few options, so you always know where you are.

## AI-safe workbooks

Workbooks made by the Laya Excel agents are **AI-safe**. They remember their own structure, so a later change never breaks them, even in a new chat on a different day. Each one has:

- a **README** sheet explaining what the workbook is for and what each column means;
- your **data sheets**, with dropdowns, automatic IDs and calculated columns (shaded grey: don't type in those);
- a **Change Log** sheet listing every change, when it happened and who asked for it;
- a hidden **_ADLC** sheet, which is the workbook's memory. Please don't delete or edit it.

You can type, edit and delete rows in Excel as normal. To change the *structure* (new columns, dropdown options, formulas), upload the workbook to the **Laya Excel Expert**. Always upload the latest version from SharePoint, then save the new version over the old one. SharePoint keeps the old copies, so nothing is ever lost.

Ready-made AI-safe templates are in the **ADLC Templates** folder: Action Tracker, RAID Log, Project Plan, Simple Register and AI Use Register.

## Documents that remember where they're up to

Documents from the writing agents end with a short **ADLC Record**: the version, status, decisions so far, open questions and the next step. Upload the document next time and the agent carries on from there.

## Getting help

If an agent gets something wrong, tell it what's wrong in plain words and it will try again. If you're still stuck, contact your team's AI champion.
