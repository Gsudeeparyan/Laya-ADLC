# Which Copilot do I have?

Most people at Laya have the free version. Some have the paid licence. Both are safe to use for work, and all the main Laya agents work on both.

Microsoft has been renaming these products, so you may see them called different things. In this guide:
- **Free** means Microsoft Copilot Chat, which comes with your Microsoft 365 account.
- **Paid** means the Microsoft Copilot licence, sometimes still called Microsoft 365 Copilot.

## How to check

1. Sign in to Microsoft 365 with your work account.
2. Look at the bottom of the home page. A label sits under your name.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Copilot Chat (Basic)** | Free. No Copilot inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint or OneNote |
| **M365 Copilot (Basic)** | Free, with standard Copilot in those apps |
| **M365 Copilot (Premium)** | Paid. The full experience in those apps, with priority access |

Think you should have the paid licence but don't? Ask your manager.

## What each one can do

| | Free | Paid |
|---|---|---|
| Chat with Copilot, with the web as its source | Yes | Yes |
| Upload files and ask about them | Yes | Yes |
| Create Word, Excel and PowerPoint files through Laya's agents | Yes | Yes |
| Use the Laya agents for everyone | Yes | Yes |
| Copilot Chat in Outlook, for your own inbox, calendar and meetings | Yes | Yes |
| Copilot that already knows your emails, chats and files, in the main chat | No: upload or paste what it needs | Yes |
| Copilot inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint and OneNote | Depends on your label | Yes |
| Meeting recaps and chat summaries in Teams | No | Yes |
| The Researcher and Analyst agents | No | Yes |
| Laya Catch Me Up, Laya Meeting Recap and Laya Policy Finder | No | Yes |
| Building your own agents in Agent Builder | No | Yes |

**On the free version, you bring the information to Copilot:** upload the file or paste the text. With the paid licence, Copilot can find it for you, among the things you already have access to.

Microsoft changes these details often. If something here doesn't match what you see, tell your AI champion.

## Is it safe to use for work?

**Look for the green shield.** It sits at the top of Copilot, next to **New chat**. It means your chat has Microsoft's enterprise data protection: what you type isn't used to train the AI models, and your chat is logged within Laya's own Microsoft 365 for audit.

**No green shield?** You may be signed in with a personal account, or using a consumer version of Copilot. Stop, and don't type anything about work.

**Use only Laya's Copilot for work.** Personal accounts and other AI websites don't have these protections.

**Be careful with web search.** Copilot Chat can search the web to help answer you, and those searches go to Bing, outside the EU Data Boundary that protects the rest of your chat. They're short phrases taken from your question, not your whole chat, but keep names and member details out of questions that might go to the web. Laya's agents have web search turned off.

**Protected doesn't mean anything goes.** Your chat is logged, and Laya's rules on personal data still apply. See [the five rules](../README.md#the-five-rules).
