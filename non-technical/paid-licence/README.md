# What the paid licence adds

Your label says **M365 Copilot (Premium)**. Everything in the free version still works for you, including all the Laya agents. On top of that, you get three things.

## 1. Copilot that can find your work

In the free version, you bring the information to Copilot. With the licence, Copilot can search your emails, chats, meetings and files for you. It only sees what you already have access to.

**A word of warning:** if a file has been shared too widely, Copilot can find it. If it shows you something you shouldn't see, don't use it. Tell the IT service desk, so the sharing can be fixed.

## 2. Copilot inside your apps

| App | Good for | Prompts |
|---|---|---|
| Word | First drafts, rewriting, summarising, asking about a long document | [Word](prompts/word.md) |
| Excel | Quick analysis, formulas, charts, finding patterns | [Excel](prompts/excel.md) |
| PowerPoint | A deck from a Word document, tidying slides, speaker notes | [PowerPoint](prompts/powerpoint.md) |
| Outlook | Summarising long threads, drafting replies, coaching on tone | [Outlook](prompts/outlook.md) |
| Teams | Meeting recaps, catching up on chats | [Teams](prompts/teams.md) |
| Copilot chat | Questions across your work, plus the Researcher and Analyst agents | [Work chat](prompts/work-chat.md) |

## 3. Three extra Laya agents

| Agent | What it does | Instead of (on free) |
|---|---|---|
| **Laya Catch Me Up** | What needs you, across email, Teams and meetings | Copilot Chat in Outlook |
| **Laya Meeting Recap** | Decisions and actions from a Teams meeting, with nothing to upload | Laya Meeting Actions, with your notes |
| **Laya Policy Finder** | What Laya's approved documents say, with the exact wording and a link | Laya Document Summariser, with the policy uploaded |

## Copilot in the app, or a Laya agent?

| Use Copilot in the app for | Use a Laya agent for |
|---|---|
| A quick question or a first draft | A job with steps that must be done properly |
| Something you'll read and fix yourself | A workbook that must not break (the AI-safe format) |
| Working inside a file you already have open | Personal data (Laya PII Guard) and checking (Laya Second Check) |

## You're also a helper

Because you have the licence, you may be asked to:

- **Build agents for others.** Building agents in Agent Builder needs the licence. Everyone else just uses them. See [how to deploy the agents](../admin/deploy-agents.md).
- **Show your team what works.** Colleagues on the free version can't try what you're using, so share what you learn, and remember that they'll need a different route for the same job.

## The rules don't change

More reach brings more responsibility. You can now reach much more information in one question, so:

- **Ask for what the job needs,** not everything on a topic.
- **Don't look up colleagues.** Using Copilot to find out about a person's emails, performance or private matters isn't acceptable, even if your account can technically reach it.
- **Read the source** before relying on a summary. Copilot links to what it used, so click through.
- **The five rules still apply.** See [the five rules](../README.md#the-five-rules).
