# Copilot in Teams

Copilot's meeting answers depend on the meeting being transcribed. Follow Laya's rules on recording and transcribing **[to confirm]**, and let people know before you turn it on.

## During or after a meeting

> Recap this meeting: decisions, actions with owners and dates, and open questions.

> What did we decide about [topic]? Was anyone unsure about it?

> What were the main points of disagreement, and how were they settled?

> I joined late. What did I miss?

> Write a follow-up message I can post in the channel.

For minutes and a follow-up email with one owner on every action, use the **Laya Meeting Recap**.

## Chats and channels

> Summarise this chat from the last week. What needs me?

> What questions in this channel are still unanswered?

> Draft a reply to this thread that [says what].

## Before you use it

- **Transcripts make mistakes,** especially with names, numbers and Irish words. Check anything that matters.
- **Check actions with their owners** before you send them round.
- **Use recaps for the record, not for judging people.** Don't use Copilot to score how colleagues did in a meeting, who spoke most, or how someone seemed to feel.
- **Keep member cases general** in chat: "a member complaint", not a name.
