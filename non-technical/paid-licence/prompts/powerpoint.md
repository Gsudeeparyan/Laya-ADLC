# Copilot in PowerPoint

## Start a deck

> Create a presentation from [/Word document] for [who], who need to decide [what]. Keep it to [number] slides. Make each title a full sentence that states the main point.

> Create a five-slide outline on [topic]: the situation, the problem, the options, my recommendation, and the next steps.

## Improve a deck

> Rewrite each slide title so it says the point of the slide, not just the topic.

> This slide has too much text. Keep the three most important points and move the rest to the speaker notes.

> Add speaker notes to this slide, as I'd say it, in under a minute.

> Summarise this deck in three sentences for someone who missed the meeting.

> What questions would [role] ask after this deck?

## Before you use it

- **Check every number and claim** against your source. Slides travel: people forward them without you there to explain.
- **Use Laya's template** so it looks right. Copilot can't always match the branding on its own.
- **Read the titles alone, in order.** If they don't tell the story, the deck isn't finished. The **Laya Presentation Builder** works this way from the start.
- **Keep member and staff details off the slides,** and check any images it adds.
