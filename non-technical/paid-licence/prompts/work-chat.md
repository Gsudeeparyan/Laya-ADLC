# Work chat, Researcher and Analyst

In Microsoft Copilot, choose the **Work** option to ask questions across your emails, chats, meetings and files.

## Find things

> Find the latest version of the [topic] document and tell me what changed since [date].

> What have I said to [team] about [topic] over the last month?

> Which documents have I been working on for [project]? Give me links.

## Get ready

> I'm meeting [role] about [topic] tomorrow. Pull together what's been agreed, what's open, and the documents I should read.

> Draft my update for [meeting] from what I've worked on this week. Link each point to its source.

## Researcher and Analyst

These are built-in agents for bigger jobs, and they take longer to answer.

**Researcher** gathers information from your work and the web into a report.

> Research how [topic] has been handled across our documents and on the public web. Keep the two separate, and give the source for every point.

**Analyst** works through data step by step and shows its working. Remove personal data first.

> Analyse this data to find what's driving the change in [measure]. Show your steps, and tell me what you couldn't conclude from it.

## Before you use it

- **Ask for what you need.** Copilot can reach everything you can, so be specific.
- **Click the links.** Copilot shows what it used, and it can read something out of context or find an old version.
- **Keep work and web apart.** Web searches go outside the EU Data Boundary, so keep names and member details out of questions that may search the web.
- **Don't look up colleagues.** Your account's access isn't permission to use it that way.
- **Check research reports** against the sources before you pass them on. They read well even when they're wrong.
