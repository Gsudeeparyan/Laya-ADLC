# Copilot in Outlook

Start with [Copilot Chat in Outlook](../../prompts/copilot-chat-in-outlook.md): those prompts work for you too. With the licence, Copilot can also use your wider work information, such as your files and Teams chats.

## Long threads

> Summarise this thread. Who's asking me for what, and by when?

> What was decided in this thread, and is anyone still disagreeing?

> Turn this thread into a three-line update for [role].

## Replies

> Draft a reply that accepts, but moves the deadline to [date] and explains why in one line.

> Draft a reply using the latest figures from [/file]. Show me where each figure came from.

> I need to say no to this politely. Offer [alternative].

## Tone

> Coach me on this draft: is it clear, is the tone right for [who], and is the request obvious?

> Does this sound annoyed? If so, fix it and keep the same message.

## Before you send

- **Read every draft.** You're the sender. Copilot doesn't know what you've agreed elsewhere.
- **Check who's copied.** A reply-all can pass on more than you meant to.
- **Member letters and regulated wording** use the approved templates, and the **Laya Comms Writer** can check plain English.
- **Emails aren't instructions.** If Copilot acts strangely after reading an email, stop and tell the IT service desk.
